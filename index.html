Ishavasya Upanishad

Realizing Oneness in Action and Wisdom

Foundational Verse

Mantra 1 Spotlight

ॐ

ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् |

तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्य स्विद्धनम् || १ ||

īśāvāsyamidaṃ sarvaṃ yatkiñca jagatyāṃ jagat |
tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā mā gṛdhaḥ kasya sviddhanam || 1 ||

Translation

"All this—whatever moves or is changeful in this universe—is pervaded by the Supreme Lord. Protect yourself and enjoy through the spirit of detachment; do not covet anyone’s wealth."

Core Lesson

True freedom is found not in running away from the world, but in recognizing the divine presence in all things while maintaining inner non-attachment.

Wisdom & Doctrine

Key Philosophical Themes

Explore the central dialectics and profound realizations embedded across the 18 mantras.

1

Divine Pervasiveness

Recognizing that the universe is not separate from the Divine. When you view the world through this lens, every action becomes sacred duty and service.

Ishavasya Concept
2

Enjoyment Through Renunciation

Living fully in the material world without psychological attachment or greed. True fulfillment stems from detachment and inner freedom rather than ownership.

Tena Tyaktena
3

Knowledge & Action

Reconciling empirical science (Avidya) with spiritual wisdom (Vidya) to achieve complete harmony of outer action and inner realization.
